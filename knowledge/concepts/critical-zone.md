# Critical Zone

The **Critical Zone** is the [Zone](/concepts/zone.md) currently selected as having the largest [Zone Error](/concepts/zone-error.md); it drives [Plant](/concepts/plant.md) demand for that control step.

Avoid “master zone” or “primary room”.

Chosen by the [Multi-Zone Coordinator](/concepts/multi-zone-coordinator.md).
